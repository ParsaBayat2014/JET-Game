# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in JET a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

Be constructive, specific and patient. Welcome different viewpoints, assume good faith, and focus criticism on the work rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through a GitHub private channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and may result in a warning, removal of content or exclusion from the project.
